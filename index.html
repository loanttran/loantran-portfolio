I want to listen to users’ experiences. Through the nuance, I want to learn what matters to them. To look at data and ask: What is the story here? And turn discoveries into actionable insights that help design and improve products and services in a rapidly changing world.

Selected work

  • 01 Payment Authentication Setup Concept Testing Three rounds of unmoderated qualitative testing on a payment authentication setup experience. Iterations focused on CTA button copy and presenting enough context for users without overburdening them with information. n = 27 3 rounds UK + US Unmoderated concept testing A/B qual AI-assisted transcript analysis
  • 02 Product Flows Worksheet Redesign The design file was the team's primary tool and "source of truth" for aligning clients and regional partners on payment authentication flows. Exploration of redesign was meant to accommodate future product scaling so the worksheet could better support different users: product teams, design teams, sales, vendors, partners, and external clients. n = 7 Generative interviews Synthesis Prototyping with Claude Code
  • 03 Agentic Commerce & Payment Authentication What are users' thoughts when it comes to AI-assisted purchases? A moderated qualitative study to understand users' needs, expectations, and concerns around AI-assisted commerce and payment authentication. n = 24 4 countries 90-min sessions Moderated qual Counterbalanced
  • 04 Security Animation at Checkout Survey An A/B/C between-subjects test of two security animations against no animation at all, fielded simultaneously in two markets. The goal was to understand whether the animations increased users' trust in the checkout experience and their likelihood to complete a purchase. n = 1,800 US + India A/B/C design Significance testing Dual-market fielding
