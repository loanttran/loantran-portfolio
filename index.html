How I work

Methods & capabilities

Most research fails in synthesis, not in fieldwork. A finding that sounds compelling and is supported by two participants will travel further through an organization than a boring finding supported by nine — unless the method makes that impossible.

So I build verification into the process rather than trusting my own read of a session. Every takeaway gets checked against the full participant set before it is elevated to a pattern. Transcripts follow a documented cleaning convention and are frozen once finalized. Codebooks are written down, applied consistently, and shipped with their limitations attached. The point is that someone else could pick up the same data and reach the same conclusions.
